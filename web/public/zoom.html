Install in four steps

The steps below are for the desktop app. On a phone, install from the store and open it.

  1. Download the installer

    Choose your platform above. The file is saved to your Downloads folder.

  2. Open the file

    Double-click it. On Windows, allow the prompt. On Mac, open the .pkg and continue through the installer.

  3. Sign in or join

    Sign in with your account, or choose Join and enter a meeting ID. No account is needed to join.

  4. Test audio and video

    Open Settings, then Audio and Video, to check your mic, speakers and camera before your first call.

System requirements

Windows 10 or 11, a dual-core processor, and 4 GB of RAM or more. A webcam and microphone are needed to share audio and video.

A recent macOS release on an Intel or Apple silicon Mac. Grant camera, microphone and screen recording access when asked.

A current version of iOS or Android with free storage for the app. Wi-Fi or a 4G/5G connection is recommended for video.

Exact minimum versions change over time. Check Zoom's official system requirements page for the latest.
